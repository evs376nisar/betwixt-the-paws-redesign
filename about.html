The Betwixt the Paws Story

Where Compassion Meets Championship Precision

"Dedicated to my passion and adoration for animal care, I became a professional Pet Groomer in 1999. I am a certified professional dog and cat groomer through the JKL Training Academy."

I provided loving, artistic care to dogs and cats first in the Fargo-Moorhead, ND metro and now proudly in the Dickinson, ND area.

While in Fargo, I trained under three professional show groomers on advanced techniques for show and family pets alike. For the last few years, I have been concentrating on the grooming, conditioning, and selective breeding of my AKC Siberian Huskies.

Care Standards

Why Dickinson Pet Parents Trust Robin

We operate our salon with uncompromising standards of safety, compassion, and artistic excellence.

100% Cage-Free Drying

We never use cage dryers or leave pets unattended in crates. Every pet is hand-fluff-dried with low-noise warm air velocity dryers while Robin stays with them every single second.

Double Coat & Husky Mastery

As an AKC Siberian Husky breeder, Robin understands the intricate anatomy of double coats. We never shave double coats unless medically required, preserving their natural thermal barrier.

Certified Cat Grooming

Cats require specialized physiological handling techniques and calm environments. Robin provides low-stress lion cuts, dry baths, and nail trims without sedation or trauma.
