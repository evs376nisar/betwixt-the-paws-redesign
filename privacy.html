Privacy Policy & SMS Opt-In Notice

How Betwixt the Paws Grooming LLC protects your personal details, pet health records, and mobile communications.

Last Updated: January 2026 TCPA & 10DLC Compliant

1. Commitment to Client Privacy

At Betwixt the Paws Grooming LLC ("we," "our," or "us"), owned and operated by Robin Fettig in Dickinson, North Dakota, we respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding the personal and pet-related information you share with us. This policy details how we collect, store, and utilize information gathered through our website (betwixtthepaws.com), physical intake forms, phone consultations, and electronic messaging.

2. Information We Collect

To provide safe, professional grooming services, we collect:

  • Client Identification: Full name, physical residential address, email address, and mobile phone number.
  • Pet Health & Profile: Pet name, age, breed, weight, medical conditions, behavioral history, and veterinarian clinic contact details.
  • Vaccination Verification: Mandatory Rabies certificate details, expiration dates, and veterinary clinic confirmation as required by North Dakota regulations.
  • Appointment Notes: Grooming preferences, coat styling instructions, hand-scissoring preferences, and past visit summaries.

3. SMS / Mobile Messaging Privacy Policy & Terms

Strict No-Sharing Guarantee:

"No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties."

SMS Opt-In Details

By checking the SMS consent box on our VIP Waitlist form, contact forms, or by signing our physical Grooming Intake Form, you authorize Betwixt the Paws Grooming LLC to send transactional and operational SMS text messages to your phone number. These messages include:

  • Appointment confirmations and reminder notifications.
  • Estimated pick-up readiness alerts (approx. 15-20 minutes before groom completion).
  • VIP waitlist opening alerts and scheduling availability.
  • Annual rabies vaccination expiration reminder notices.

Opt-Out & Help Procedures

• How to Opt-Out: You may cancel the SMS service at any time. Simply reply STOP, CANCEL, QUIT, END, or UNSUBSCRIBE to any text message received from us. You will receive one confirmation of cancellation, after which no further SMS messages will be sent unless re-requested.

• How to Get Help: If you experience issues or have questions regarding messaging, reply with the keyword HELP, or contact us directly at (701) 690-1572 or via email at groomer@betwixtthepaws.com.

• Rates & Carrier Disclaimer: Message and data rates may apply for any messages sent to you from us and to us from you. Message frequency varies according to your appointment scheduling. Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

4. How We Use Your Information

Your information is exclusively used for legitimate pet care and business operations:

  • To safely customize grooming techniques to your pet's physical conditions, arthritis, skin sensitivities, or age.
  • To maintain state-mandated rabies compliance records.
  • To reach emergency contacts or your designated veterinarian clinic in the rare event of a sudden medical situation during care.
  • To coordinate drop-off and pickup times.

5. Data Protection & Security

We implement administrative and technical measures to protect your personal details against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. Physical forms and medical cards are securely stored within our private salon facility, and electronic correspondence is transmitted over encrypted channels. We do not sell, rent, or monetize client lists under any circumstance.

6. Photography & Social Media Release

From time to time, Robin photographs finished haircuts to showcase our salon craftsmanship in our Furry Gallery or social media. We never publish owner names, addresses, or phone numbers alongside pet photographs without explicit written consent. If you prefer that your pet not be photographed, simply let Robin know on your intake form.
