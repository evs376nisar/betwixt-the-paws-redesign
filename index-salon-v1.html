Meet Your Groomer

25+ Years Devoted to Animal Care

"Dedicated to my passion and adoration for animal care, I became a professional Pet Groomer in 1999. I am a certified professional dog and cat groomer through the JKL Training Academy."

Robin first provided loving, artistic care to dogs and cats in the Fargo-Moorhead, ND metro before establishing her dedicated retreat in the Dickinson, ND area.

While in Fargo, Robin apprenticed directly under three professional show groomers, mastering championship show coat conditioning alongside practical family lifestyle cuts. For the last several years, she has also concentrated on the grooming, conditioning, and selective breeding of her AKC Siberian Huskies.

“

The gentle, skilled care that my own dogs have flourished under, I now provide to your fur babies. Remember, no job is too big or too small for me to handle.

— Robin Fettig, Groomer & Manager
