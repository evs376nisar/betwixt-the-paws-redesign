First Appointment Grooming Forms

To simplify your pet's first visit, please review the requirements below. You can download the forms to print and bring in, or complete your details online in advance.

Proof of Current Rabies Vaccination Strictly Required

Don't forget to bring proof of current rabies vaccination for your pet! By North Dakota health regulations and our salon safety protocol, all canine and feline guests must have verified, current rabies immunization prior to appointment intake.

How to Prepare for Your 1st Appointment:

  1. Download or Fill Online: Download the Word documents below to print, fill out by hand, and bring to your appointment.
  2. Veterinary Immunization Records: Bring your official rabies certificate or have your veterinary clinic email records directly to groomer@betwixtthepaws.com prior to arrival.
  3. Leash & Carrier Safety: For the safety of all animals, dogs must be on a secure leash and cats must arrive in a comfortable, secure carrier.
  4. Special Instructions: Notify Robin of any behavioral nervousness, joint stiffness, skin allergies, or medical sensitivities during intake.
