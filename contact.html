COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about scheduling, salon policies, and appointment procedures.

Q: Why is Betwixt the Paws appointment-only?

Unlike noisy commercial grooming mills where dozens of dogs bark in cages for hours waiting their turn, Robin provides individualized, one-on-one cage-free care. By operating strictly by appointment, each pet receives focused, continuous attention and goes home as soon as they are finished.

Q: What vaccines does my pet need before coming in?

North Dakota state regulation and salon policy require that all dogs and cats have a valid, up-to-date Rabies vaccination. Bordetella (kennel cough) and DHPP are strongly recommended for dogs.

Q: What is your cancellation or rescheduling policy?

Because Robin reserves a private dedicated time window for your pet, we require a minimum of 24 hours notice to cancel or reschedule without penalty. This allows waitlisted clients to fill the opening.

Q: Can I wait in the salon during my dog's haircut?

In our 25+ years of experience, pets are significantly calmer and safer when owners are not in the room, as pets will jump, wiggle, and cry to get to their owner while sharp shears are in use. Robin will call or text you approximately 15-20 minutes before completion for pickup!
