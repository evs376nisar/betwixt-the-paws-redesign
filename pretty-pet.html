Dedicated To Your Pet's Health & Happiness

Founded by certified master groomer Robin Fettig, who graduated from the prestigious JKL Training Academy in 1999 and trained under premier show-dog mentors in Fargo-Moorhead. As an AKC Siberian Husky breeder with over 25 years of hands-on expertise, Robin provides personalized one-on-one pet care in a safe, clean, and calm environment.

Whether it's a routine coat blowout or a complex breed scissor cut, we ensure every visit is 100% cage-free, low-stress, and deeply enjoyable for your beloved four-legged companion.
